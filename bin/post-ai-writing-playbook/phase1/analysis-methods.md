## T00–T03 — Extraction, calibration, and cohort statistics

Scan D02 candidates in ascending normalized ID order. Verify the original byte hashes of successfully retrieved HTML. Assign separate states for non-HTML responses, acquisition failures, and missing body text, and distinguish them from empty S. For the first probe, use the five fixed D02 candidates. Use synthetic fixtures for any of successful acquisition, empty prose, or failure that the candidates lack. Shard extraction and verify hashes for completed shards at the next start. Preserve the extractor's `prose_text` and units exactly.

For T01 calibration, sort candidates from each year by `SHA256('42:language:'+article_id)`. Freeze the first 20 for calibration and the next 10 for validation. In a separate diagnostic sample, select up to five in the same order for each category of empty text, text shorter than 180 characters, disagreement with the HTML language hint, and more than 50 non-ASCII characters. Record actual counts and reasons for shortfalls. Hide year, group, and scores. Read all of short texts or the first, middle, and last 210 characters of longer texts. Read full S before overriding mixed or unknown classifications. Freeze thresholds and calibration results before assessing the 80 validation cases. Save the confusion matrix, incorrect English inclusions, indeterminate cases, original decisions, and overrides with reasons.

For T02, sort included articles within each year by `SHA256('42:segmentation:'+article_id)` and freeze the first ten for calibration. Use the first 16 hexadecimal digits of `SHA256('42:segmentation-blind:'+article_id)` as the blinded ID. Select one P owning paragraph by `SHA256('42:segmentation-block:'+blind_id+':'+block_id)`. Keep the shortfall for any article without P. Freeze blinded boundary labels before opening machine output. Record boundary precision and recall with terminal boundaries excluded, and report measured values alongside the original run's 95% target for each. [t02_controls.json](references/t02_controls.json) supplies separate controls for the known original rules. Diagnose potential untagged code and structural anomalies separately. Check metadata against the source HTML and provenance. Include organization-associated posts, coauthors, profile-only cases, and unknowns as negative controls. Report agreement as an observed sample rate.

T03 summarizes counts at each stage from candidacy through acquisition, extraction, language detection, and inclusion. Report P/L/U/S totals and quantiles, word-count bands, year/month/quarter coverage, missing values for fields such as author and tags, counts of identical text, and date inconsistencies. Compute continuous quantiles by linearly interpolating position `(n−1)×p` in ascending order. Use S word-count bands of 0, 1–49, 50–99, 100–299, 300–999, and 1,000 or more. Use separate ECDF denominators for all articles and the articles with positive word counts.

## A01 — Article and paragraph lengths

Use [a01-settings.json](references/a01-settings.json). Retain zero P and S word counts. Calculate yearly means, medians, quantiles, and shares by word-count band, the year-equally-weighted primary M01 comparison, additional differences on the original scale, and matched January–September differences. Calculate each article's median P owning-block word count. Give each paragraph equal weight for one quantile calculation and divide each article's weight equally across its owning paragraphs for a second. To review long articles, take the union of the top 1% by P and S within each year. Inspect two per year in hash order, checking beginning, middle, and end, possible code, and incorrect joins.

## A03 — Lists, bold spans, and headings

Select a fixed set of quality candidates per year and use the original DOM to distinguish structures outside S from candidates still present in S.

## A04 — Vocabulary discovery and validation

Use [a04-settings.json](references/a04-settings.json). Join nonempty P texts in source unit order with two newlines and calculate SHA-256. Across all target years, join numeric authors with an edge when they share at least one hash, and calculate connected components. Use the lowest numeric ID as the known-author component key `dev:user:<id>`. Assign components to discovery if the first byte of `SHA256('42:vocab:'+component_key)` is below 128, and to validation otherwise. Include unknown authors only in overall summaries. Keep articles with empty P and exclude empty P from edge construction.

Lowercase the saved ASCII word forms and count 1–5-grams in P. Use unstemmed forms and retain stop words. Break at source unit, owning-block, machine sentence, and non-word-token boundaries. Punctuation alone does not break a sequence. If the saved word sequence lacks boundaries for removed inline code, record that phrases can span them and pass that limitation to V01.

A phrase qualifies if it occurs in at least 20 articles and by at least ten authors across the primary comparison years in discovery. Yearly denominators include all articles in the group, including zero-word P. Select 30 increases and 30 decreases by absolute difference with equal year weights. Break ties by shorter n and then lexicographic order. Select every qualifying phrase with a baseline rate of at most 0.1%, comparison rate of at least 1%, and at least 20 comparison authors as previously rare. Track the watch words `delve`, `robust`, and `seamless` separately. Freeze the candidates and selection table by hash, then count candidate phrases in P, L, and H separately in validation.

For every original candidate, show same direction, reverse direction, zero, or NA in validation. Report whether the previously rare phrases reached the thresholds again. Use separate columns for document prevalence, mean article occurrences per 1,000 words, total occurrences divided by total words, author prevalence, and top-one and top-five author shares. Set the ratio to NA when baseline prevalence is zero. For conditional validation intervals, sample known-author components across years and require 30 components in each comparison year. Omit p-values for the exploratory vocabulary analysis. Compare within fixed topic and P word-count bands only with at least 20 articles and ten authors in every year. Report MATTR-100/50 on the eligible populations and window shortfall by year and word-count band. Distinguish verified zero leakage of exact nonempty P matches in the full cohort from the possible effects of near duplicates.

Freeze the 15 largest increases and 15 largest decreases in each window. Use A04 boundary and tie rules. Report validation results, concentration in identical texts, and spread across authors.

## A07 — Experience and concrete descriptions

Use [a07-sample-settings.json](references/a07-sample-settings.json) to sort articles by `SHA256('42:evidence:'+article_id)` within each year. Freeze the first four for calibration and the next 16 for evaluation, up to 32 and 128 respectively. Show the title, body, and reference context. Hide the year, author, group, URL, and ID mapping. Open evaluation texts after freezing calibration and the proxy dictionary. Keep any sample shortfall. Verify full-text review explicitly.

Use frozen S sentences with positive word counts as the judgment units. Assign present, absent, or uncertain for each type. Save the evidence, sentence ID, supporting sentence references, and rationale.

| Type | Included content | Decision boundaries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Action or observation | Reported technical actions or observations, descriptions of implemented work, results observed during completed procedures, and actions by specific third parties | Exclude instructions, predictions, and generic capabilities. Record firsthand actions, implementation descriptions, completed procedures, and third-party reports by mode. |
| Failure and response | A problem that occurred and a response carried out | Count one instance on the response sentence and link to the problem ID. Exclude generic workarounds and future plans. |
| Quantity and conditions | A measured quantity about the author or team's actual subject, with a period, input, environment, attempt, or cumulative scope | Exclude versions, discounts, identifiers, expected outputs, and measurements attributed solely to third parties. |
| Constraints and counterexamples | Applicability limits, unsupported cases, concrete conditions for success, drawbacks, and counterexamples | Exclude general installation requirements and restatements of specifications or procedures. |
| Sourced claims | A specific claim associated with a source, official documentation, code, or a named person | Exclude bare URLs and reference-link lists. Record attribution without evaluating source accuracy. |

Record quoted or out-of-S evidence as context only. At article level, use present if any qualifying instance is present, uncertain when none is present and at least one is uncertain, and absent otherwise. Use the present count as the lower bound and present plus uncertain as the upper bound. Divide by the article's S word count for density and use NA for zero words. Save multiple-label overlaps and the union. Before seeing results for the 128 cases, freeze four per year for rerating in another session. Hide the initial judgments during rerating and adjudicate differences afterward. Report same-model reratings as such.

Write a separate output for the 25 full-cohort proxies in [a07-proxy-settings-v1.json](references/a07-proxy-settings-v1.json). Count leftmost-longest phrase matches within P sentences, numeric candidates with frozen units, links, and structures according to the definitions. Cross-tabulate proxies and content on the same evaluation articles and read evidence for false positives, false negatives, and unknowns. A05 uses P word counts and content labels use S word counts. Report year, word-count band, primary body-purpose category, and upper and lower bounds for indeterminate cases. Mark unsupported intervals NA with a reason. Report proxy frequencies as proxy frequencies.

## A09 — Composition and authors

Build cells from primary topic by P word-count band. Define each metric's common support as cells with at least 30 total articles and at least 30 valid observations for that metric in each of the six primary comparison years. Calculate `q_c=mean_baseline(N_yc/N_y)` from all baseline articles and freeze `p_mc` by normalizing within that metric's common support. Annual standardized values are `Σ_c p_mc × cell_mean_ycm`. Assign `p_mc/n_ycm` to each article. Record annual total weight of 1, excluded mass, ESS, weight multipliers below 0.1 or above 10, and article weights above 0.01. Compare against unadjusted values for the same population. Conditioning M01 on P word-count band changes the estimand.

For known authors, calculate means within each year and author first. Compare each year's equal-author-weight mean against the equal-article-weight mean for the same population. For authors with valid years in both periods, give equal weight to each author's observed years within the period, calculate within-author differences, and report the equal-author-weight mean, median, sign, and selection share. Distinguish the common-support population from all valid articles. Let `p0,p1` be the equal-year-weight cell masses and `v0,v1` the cell means before and after. Verify the identity `Δ=Σ[(p1−p0)(v1+v0)/2+(p1+p0)(v1−v0)/2]`. Use period conditional means consistent with the equal-year-weight cell masses. Interpret contributions as an arithmetic decomposition.

For additional A04–A08 comparisons, preserve each task's groups, content samples, proxies, and eligible domains and compare within the same populations. Keep support and weights frozen during known-author resampling. Record the relationship between missing cells and results in each replicate. Detect candidate body AI phrases using length-preserving normalization and within-S-unit matches from [a09-ai-topic.json](references/a09-ai-topic.json), preserving source character offsets. Store tag decisions separately from body phrase decisions, and distinguish unobserved tags, empty S, and missing inputs.

## A10 — Timing, fixed boundaries, and exploratory change points

Use [a10-methods.json](references/a10-methods.json). Build year, month, and quarter grids per metric and population, marking partial and unobserved months in 2025. Include all English articles, known authors, common cells, fixed q, the group with valid update timestamps, and four strata for CC capture delay. If any positive-weight fixed-q cell has a valid n of zero, return NA and leave surviving weights unchanged.

A supported month has complete coverage, at least 100 English articles, at least 100 valid observations, and at least 30 known authors. Eligible windows require at least 36 supported months before the boundary and 18 after, with no run of three or more unsupported months on either side. If monthly support fails, try quarterly thresholds of 200/200/50, at least 12 supported quarters before the boundary and six after, and no run of two or more unsupported quarters. Rank by longest calendar scope, most supported periods, earliest start, and latest end, in that order. Freeze the window using support alone and fall back to annual summaries when no candidate qualifies.

Use January 2023 as the fixed boundary. Fit OLS with `1, calendar t, post, post×(t−boundary), seasonal dummies`. Exclude November and December 2022 from the fixed-boundary fit and keep both in the observed data table. Construct Bartlett HAC only from residual-score pairs separated by exactly h calendar periods. Save monthly lags 12 and 6, quarterly lag 4, correction `n/(n−k)`, and t intervals with `n−k` degrees of freedom. Keep calendar gaps intact. Test placebo boundaries in 2020 and 2021 on the pre-period alone. For the frozen extrapolation check, train on 2018–2020 and score from 2021 through October 2022. Keep the row when support for complete periods is insufficient. Compare composition on a common month mask and report population-specific masks separately.

On the same supported calendar mask, compare the seasonal no-change-point model with every candidate one-change-point model allowing a step and a slope change. Each candidate requires 18 observations on either side. Use `BIC=n ln(RSS/n)+k ln(n)`. Use k of 13 and 16 for monthly data and 5 and 8 for quarterly data, including one parameter for location in the one-change-point model. Keep ties within `1e−10`. Resample centered residuals from the selected model in contiguous, non-circular blocks with replacement. Fill each contiguous observed span separately and keep the missingness mask fixed. Reevaluate candidates in 1,000 replicates for each monthly block length of 6 and 12 and quarterly length of 2 and 4. Keep failed replicate IDs. When `ΔBIC<6`, report the change point as unidentified. Apply the configured conditional stability criteria for resampling and describe date ranges as conditional results.
